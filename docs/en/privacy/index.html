Days Remember Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Days Remember (时光) is an anniversary and countdown app. We do not collect or sell any of your personal information; your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Data we collect

None. Days Remember has no account and no server of its own. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.

Where your data is stored

  • The days, categories, notes, places and photos you add are stored on your iPhone, where the app and its widgets read them.
  • If your device is signed in to iCloud, they sync through Apple’s CloudKit to the private database of your own iCloud account, and your reminder settings sync through iCloud key-value storage. The developer cannot access this data in your iCloud.
  • Recently deleted days, and versions replaced during sync, are kept on each device rather than in iCloud, and are removed with their photos after 30 days.
  • Before restoring a backup or applying changes from sync, the app keeps a copy of your data as it was on the device, for Restore Previous Local Copy, until the next one replaces it.
  • Backup files you export are saved wherever you choose. They are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to schedule anniversary reminders and the daily greeting on your device. Nothing goes through a server.
  • Add to Photos: used only when you tap Save, to add a share card to your photo library. The app does not read your library.
  • Photos: through the system photo picker, only the photo you choose is given to the app, as a cover.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: when you ask about or open a day with Siri, Shortcuts or Spotlight, the app reads that day on your device to answer.

Crash reports

If you choose to share with app developers in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports. Whether to share is up to you; the app itself does not collect them.

Deleting your data

When you delete a day in the app, it is also deleted from your iCloud and your other devices if your device is signed in to iCloud. Each device keeps it in its own Recently Deleted for 30 days, and the devices that receive the deletion also keep it in Saved Sync Versions, which are cleared after 30 days too. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device, but not the copy in your iCloud; to remove that, delete the days in the app first, or delete Days Remember’s data from iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

Children

Days Remember collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
