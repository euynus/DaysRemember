Features

Keep the days that matter on your Home Screen, with gentle reminders

  • Lunar calendar and solar terms

    Repeat a day every year by the Gregorian or the lunar calendar. The calendar shows lunar dates, the 24 solar terms and traditional festivals, with lunar dates from 1900 through 2100.

    24solar terms

  • Hand-drawn covers

    Give a day a photo, or pick one of the hand-drawn illustrations. A new day starts with a cover that suits its category.

    12illustrations

  • Reminders

    Get reminded 7, 3 or 1 day ahead, or on the day, at a time you choose. The Daily Greeting and Memories can be turned on separately.

    7 · 3 · 1days before

  • Widgets

    Small, medium and large on the Home Screen, plus three Lock Screen styles. Each widget can show a day of its own.

    6styles

  • Share cards

    Photo, Journal, Minimal and Split templates, with a 3:4 portrait export. Notes stay off the image unless you include them.

    4templates

  • Custom categories

    Besides Love, Family, Travel, Work and Life, create categories of your own, each with its own icon and color.

    5colors

  • iCloud sync

    Days, categories and photos sync across your devices through your own iCloud. A deleted day stays on the device for 30 days, so you can restore it.

    30days to restore
