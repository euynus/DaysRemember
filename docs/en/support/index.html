Questions

Which time zone are countdowns based on?

Beijing time. Days turn over at midnight Beijing time, and reminders arrive by Beijing time too.

How do I make a widget show a particular day?

Touch and hold the widget on your Home Screen, choose Edit Widget, then pick a day. Without a choice, the widget shows the nearest upcoming day, or the most recent past day when nothing is ahead. In the app, Me › Widgets is only a preview.

Why didn’t a reminder arrive?

First check that notifications are on in iOS Settings › Notifications › Days Remember. iOS lets an app schedule only a limited number of reminders in advance, so Days Remember schedules the nearest ones first. If you haven’t opened the app for a long time, open it once to schedule the rest. Me › Reminder Settings shows how many reminders iOS has scheduled.

Will my days move to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and your days, categories and photos sync over. You can also use Export Backup in Me › Data and Sync, then Restore from File on the new device; a backup can be up to 100 MB.

Can I get back a day I deleted?

Yes. Me › Data and Sync › Recently Deleted keeps deleted days for 30 days. The deletion syncs to your other devices, and each keeps its own copy, so you can restore the day on any of them.

What if I edit the same day on two devices?

Nothing is lost. The replaced version is kept for 30 days in Me › Data and Sync › Saved Sync Versions, where you can restore it.

Why are some days under Past?

A one-time day moves from Ahead to Past once its date has gone by, and counts up from then on. A yearly day always stays in Ahead, counting down to its next occurrence.

How do I add a lunar day?

When you create or edit a day, set Calendar to Lunar and Type to Yearly. The day then repeats by the lunar calendar. Lunar dates are supported from 1900 through 2100.

How do I change the language?

In Me › Language, choose Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English or System Default.
