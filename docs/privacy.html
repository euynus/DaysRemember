时光隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日

时光（Days Remember）是一款纪念日与倒数 App。我们不收集、不储存、也不出售你的任何个人信息。

我们收集的数据

没有。时光没有账号，没有自己的服务器，不含广告、统计分析或追踪代码，也没有接入任何第三方 SDK。

你的数据存放在哪里

  • 你记录的日子、分类、笔记、地点和照片保存在你的 iPhone 上，时光和它的小组件都从这里读取。
  • 如果你的设备登录了 iCloud，这些内容会通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 同步到你自己 iCloud 账户的私有数据库，提醒设置通过 iCloud 键值存储同步。开发者无法访问你 iCloud 中的这些数据。
  • 最近删除的日子和同步时被替换的版本只保存在这台设备上，30 天后自动清除。
  • 你导出的备份文件保存在你选择的位置。备份文件没有加密，请放在可信的地方。

权限

  • 通知：只用于在设备上安排纪念日提醒和每日问候，不经过任何服务器。
  • 添加到相册：只在你点「保存」时，把分享卡片存入相册。时光不读取你的相册。
  • 照片：通过系统照片选择器，只有你选中的那一张照片会交给时光，用作封面。

崩溃报告

如果你在 iOS「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 分析与改进」中选择与 App 开发者共享，Apple 可能会向开发者提供匿名的崩溃报告。是否共享由你决定，时光本身不收集这些信息。

删除数据

在 App 中删除一个日子，它也会从你的 iCloud 和其他设备上删除（这台设备的「最近删除」会保留 30 天）。删除 App 会移除这台设备上的全部数据。

儿童

时光不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页并修改生效日期。

联系我们

有任何问题，请在 GitHub 上提交，或查看支持页面。

Days Remember Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Days Remember (时光) is an anniversary and countdown app. We do not collect, store or sell any of your personal information.

Data we collect

None. Days Remember has no account and no server of its own. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.

Where your data is stored

  • The days, categories, notes, places and photos you add are stored on your iPhone, where the app and its widgets read them.
  • If your device is signed in to iCloud, they sync through Apple’s CloudKit to the private database of your own iCloud account, and your reminder settings sync through iCloud key-value storage. The developer cannot access this data in your iCloud.
  • Recently deleted days, and versions replaced during sync, are kept only on the device and are removed after 30 days.
  • Backup files you export are saved wherever you choose. They are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to schedule anniversary reminders and the daily greeting on your device. Nothing goes through a server.
  • Add to Photos: used only when you tap Save, to add a share card to your photo library. The app does not read your library.
  • Photos: through the system photo picker, only the photo you choose is given to the app, as a cover.

Crash reports

If you choose to share with app developers in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports. Whether to share is up to you; the app itself does not collect them.

Deleting your data

When you delete a day in the app, it is also deleted from your iCloud and your other devices (Recently Deleted keeps it on this device for 30 days). Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

Children

Days Remember collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For any question, open an issue on GitHub or see the support page.
